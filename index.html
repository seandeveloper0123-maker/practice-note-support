Start with a small passage

Add a piece and its movements, create a task in your own words, and start practice. Save a note or a take whenever it helps. Your session remains connected to the piece and task.

Record, listen, compare

Use the microphone to record a take. Choose two saved takes for A/B listening. The drone, chord sounds, metronome with tap tempo, and tuner with a reference sound work on your device.

Microphone help

If recording or the tuner cannot hear your instrument, check Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → Practice Note. Save a recording before using the tuner. Headphones help prevent the microphone from picking up reference sounds.

Your saved practice

Practice notes, session history and recordings are saved on your device. There is no app account or app-managed cloud sync. Deleting a piece keeps its saved history and recordings. Deleting the app removes its local data; offloading the app is different and may preserve data. Apple backups may retain earlier copies.

Need help?

Email seandeveloper0123@gmail.com with your app version, device and what happened. Please avoid including sensitive notes or recordings unless necessary. We use your message to respond to your request.
